Workout & Nutrition Plan

Create and assign personalized workout and nutrition plans for members. Enhance their progress by collaborating with other coaches.

  • Design customized workout routines tailored to individual goals and fitness levels
  • Create nutrition plans to complement workout routines and maximize results
  • Share plans with other coaches and track member adherence for continuous improvement

Fitness Test

Conduct fitness assessments to measure progress and identify areas for improvement. Regular tracking helps optimize member results.

  • Perform standardized fitness tests to assess strength, endurance, and flexibility
  • Track and document the progress to monitor member improvements over time
  • Use test results to customize workouts and set achievable fitness goals

Transformation

Record and track body metrics to visualize progress and transformations over time. Help members stay motivated and achieve their fitness goals.

  • Upload progress photos to provide a visual representation of transformations
  • Use data to adjust fitness plans and celebrate member milestones effectively

Community Feed

Foster a sense of community by sharing updates, tips, and inspiration. Keep members motivated and engaged through interactive content.

  • Share member fitness journeys, progress updates, and transformation stories
  • Post expert tips, workout ideas, and motivational content to inspire members

Manage, track, and get real-time progress updates for participants of scheduled fitness challenges.

  • Track ongoing challenge progress
  • Monitor participant engagement and performance
  • Get detailed reports and insights on challenge outcomes
