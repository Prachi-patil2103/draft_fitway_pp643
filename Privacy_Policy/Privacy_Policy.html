Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1st July, 2025

At FitWay, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Information We Collect
  • Personal details such as your full name, email address, and phone number
  • Mailing or residential address provided during sign-up or communication
  • Location data, if permitted, to enhance your local experience
  • Your preferences and interests related to fitness and wellness
  • Fitness-related data such as workout history, goals, schedules, and body metrics
  • A facial image used to generate a unique “Facial Geometry” for faster check-ins (only if you opt-in)
  • Your original facial photo is automatically deleted after processing and can be removed upon request
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser version, and device type
  • Activity data including pages visited and interactions on our platform
2. How We Use Your Data
  • To provide and improve our SaaS solutions for gyms and fitness centers
  • To manage user accounts and offer customer support
  • To process payments securely using third-party providers (e.g., Stripe, Razorpay)
  • To send notifications via email/SMS
  • To remarket and lead capture through platforms like Meta (Facebook)
  • Note: We do not sell or share your personal data for profit
3. Data Storage and Security
  • All data is stored securely on servers located in India
  • Encryption, SSL technology, and malware scanning are used to protect your data
  • No method of online transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we follow best practices
4. Third-Party Services
  • Payment Processing: Stripe, Razorpay
  • Email & SMS Notifications: SendGrid, SMS Gateway Center
  • Marketing & Lead Capture: Meta
5. User Rights
  • You can access your personal data and request corrections
  • You may delete your account anytime via user settings
  • Upon deletion, data is removed from active systems
  • You can request deletion of your facial recognition data anytime via settings
6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
  • Enhance your browsing experience
  • Track performance and usage patterns
  • Save shopping cart items
  • Remember your preferences for future visits
7. CAN-SPAM Compliance
  • We do not use misleading email subjects or addresses
  • All marketing emails are clearly identified
  • You can unsubscribe via a link or by contacting us at info@thefitway.io
8. Changes to This Policy
  • We may update this policy from time to time
  • Changes will be posted with a new effective date at the top
